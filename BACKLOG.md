# Will We Get Wet? - Hackathon Backlog

- **1. Understand the observations** — Explore the data and decide what makes it suitable for your prediction.
- **2. Find enough data** — Gather enough relevant history to train and evaluate a model.
- **3. Define "getting wet"** — Choose a prediction target and time horizon that help answer the canoeing question.
- **4. Train and evaluate a model** — Build a small model and compare it with the baseline. Use this simple reference: "If it is raining now, predict rain next hour. Otherwise, predict dry." Explain the results; beating the baseline is a bonus, not a requirement.
- **5. Show a canoeing outlook** — Turn your prediction into an understandable result, making uncertainty and limitations clear.
- **6. Test and demonstrate** — Show that your approach works, explain what you learned, and distinguish a historical demonstration from a live forecast.
